Ladakh's religious landscape reflects centuries of belief, learning, travel and cultural exchange. Its monasteries and mosques are not only places of worship, but also important parts of the region's history, architecture and community life.

01

Buddhism in Ladakh

Compassion · Wisdom · The Middle Way

Tibetan Buddhism has had a deep influence on Ladakh's spiritual and cultural life for centuries. Its teachings emphasise compassion, wisdom, mindfulness and understanding the causes of suffering. Prayer flags, chortens, sacred art and monastery rituals are familiar features of many Ladakhi landscapes. Monasteries, also known as gompas, serve as places of worship, meditation, education and the preservation of traditional knowledge. They remain active centres of religious life.

Faith and daily life

A Tradition of Wisdom

Buddhist practice in Ladakh includes prayer, meditation, study and acts of compassion. Monastic communities help preserve religious texts, art, ceremonies and teachings passed from one generation to the next.

Monasteries also connect spiritual life with village communities through religious observances and annual celebrations.

Major Monasteries

Centres of faith and learning
Hemis · Near Leh

Hemis Monastery

One of Ladakh's best-known monasteries, Hemis belongs to the Drukpa lineage of Tibetan Buddhism. It is renowned for its sacred art, thangkas and annual Hemis Tsechu celebrations.

MONASTERY 01
Indus Valley · East of Leh

Thiksey Monastery

Founded in the 15th century, Thiksey is known for its hilltop architecture, monastic community and impressive statue of Maitreya Buddha.

MONASTERY 02
Nubra Valley

Diskit Monastery

An important Buddhist centre in Nubra Valley, Diskit overlooks the valley and is associated with the region's religious history and the large Maitreya Buddha statue nearby.

MONASTERY 03
Alchi · Indus Valley

Alchi Monastery

This historic monastic complex is celebrated for its remarkable murals, sculptures and woodwork, which preserve important evidence of early Himalayan and Kashmiri artistic traditions.

02

Islam in Ladakh

Faith · Community · Service

Islam is an important part of Ladakh's religious and cultural landscape, particularly in Kargil and in communities across Leh and Nubra. Muslims in the region include communities with distinct local histories and traditions. Islamic beliefs emphasise the worship of one God, prayer, charity, compassion and responsibility towards others. Mosques provide spaces for congregational prayer, religious learning and community gatherings. The history of Islam in Ladakh is also connected with trade, travel and contact with neighbouring regions.

Jama Masjid, Leh
Faith and community

A Place to Gather

The mosque holds an important place in Muslim community life. It is a place for daily prayers and congregational worship, particularly the Friday prayer, as well as religious learning and shared observances.

Ladakh's Muslim communities have contributed to the region's history, commerce, crafts and cultural life. Their mosques form an important part of the built heritage of towns and villages.

Major Mosques

Places of prayer and community
Leh · Main town

Jama Masjid

Located in the old town of Leh, Jama Masjid is an important mosque for the local Muslim community and a recognisable part of the town's religious heritage.

MOSQUE 01
Kargil

Mosques of Kargil

Kargil has a significant Muslim population and a strong tradition of religious community life. Its mosques and imambaras reflect the area's varied Islamic traditions and local architectural styles.

MOSQUE 02
Nubra Valley

Mosques of Nubra

Mosques in Nubra serve local communities across villages shaped by centuries of travel, trade and cultural contact along routes connecting Ladakh with neighbouring regions.

MOSQUE 03

Shah Hamdan Mosque
