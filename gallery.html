The
Archive.

A visual journey through Ladakh's royal past, historic settlements and sacred landscapes. Photographs and illustrations preserve moments from a region where history remains part of everyday life.

A living history.
Memory · Place · Time

One land.
Many memories.

From the palaces of Leh to the monasteries of Nubra, each image opens a window into Ladakh's architectural and cultural past. Together, they form one continuous visual record of a remarkable Himalayan landscape.

Royal architecture

Stone, time
and memory.

Rising above the old town, Leh Palace stands as a reminder of the region's royal history. Historic photographs help preserve not only its architecture, but also the changing appearance of the settlement surrounding it.

Trade & everyday life

Where journeys
once met.

For generations, Leh connected traders and travellers moving across Himalayan and Central Asian routes. Its bazaars became places of exchange, bringing together goods, languages, ideas and people from distant regions.

Nubra Valley · Sacred architecture

Above the
valley.

In Nubra, monasteries occupy a landscape shaped by mountain passes, river valleys and generations of religious practice. Diskit Gompa remains one of the region's most recognisable cultural landmarks, linking its living communities with a long Buddhist tradition.

Ladakh Heritage · Closing note

History is not only
what remains.
It is what we remember.

Buildings change, settlements grow, and generations move forward. Through photographs, illustrations and the memories attached to them, the story of Ladakh continues to be preserved.
